# 03. Korean Verb Precision & Prohibited Lexicon Policy

> **Trigger**: When communicating in Korean, writing user-facing documentation, committing messages, or formulating status logs.  

## Directives

1. **Prohibited Slang & Crude Metaphors**:
   - Strictly ban the word `박다` and its variants in technical contexts (e.g. `코드를 박아넣다`, `데이터를 박다`). Use precise technical verbs: `삽입하다`, `주입하다`, `저장하다`, `기록하다`, `배치하다`.
   - Ban non-technical mimetic expressions, bodily metaphors, and casual slang in professional/technical deliverables.

2. **Deconstruction of Light Verbs**:
   - Avoid empty nominal compounding (`진행을 하다` → `진행하다`, `처리를 하다` → `처리하다`).
